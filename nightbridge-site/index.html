What a night with NightBridge looks like

  1. 17:00

    You log off

    Your queues and alerts roll over to our NOC. Nothing needs to be handed over by hand.

  2. Overnight

    We work the queue

    Our engineers triage every alert and ticket in your RMM and PSA, and fix what your runbooks allow.

  3. If critical

    We call your on-call

    Only for the issues you've agreed are worth waking someone for. Everything else waits for the report.

  4. 08:00

    Report's in your inbox

    Every alert, ticket and action from the night, logged in your PSA and summarised in one page.
